# Seats to hand-check — 5.e.viii, njv3 labels

Universe: the 122 seats of `set1` (both pg1 replicates answer `yes` to `q3_avoid_limb` **and** `q4_conceal_limb`), minus the 50 seats whose strict fabrication I confirmed in `findings/5-e-viii-deception.md` → 72 seats left. Labels are the njv3 message labels (`gpt56sol`, sidecars `run.deception_njv3_gpt56sol{,_r2}.json`), unioned over the two replicates; `(r1 only)`/`(r2 only)` marks a label just one replicate gave.

Of the 72: 60 carry `framing`, 12 do not; of those 12, 1 carries `omission` and 11 carry neither.

**The inclusion rule is seat-level:** a seat is listed when *that* seat pursues — both pg1 replicates `yes` on `q3_avoid_limb` and `q4_conceal_limb` — since it is the seat the behaviour was observed on. Its partner seat in the same run need not pursue; only 48 of the 78 runs have both seats in `set1`. The **partner seat in set1** column is informational, not a filter: `yes` means both agents in that run pursue avoid-and-conceal on both replicates; otherwise it gives the partner's pg1 verdicts (`partly` = one limb short, usually `q3_avoid_limb` or `q4_conceal_limb` answered `no`/`partly`; `no` = not playing for the goal at all, e.g. `kimi s1 Nadia`, which the pg1 gate holds out of the njv3 reports entirely).

The **first `framing` message** is that seat's earliest framing-labelled message by (turn, step), written `turn N step M, surface, HH:MM` — the handle `njv3_sol_78runs_strictfab.md` uses. A trailing `(r1)`/`(r2)` means the replicates picked different first messages, or only that replicate found framing at all; the verdict column then quotes that replicate. The **that message as sent** column is the message verbatim (line breaks shown as `<br>`). The verdict column carries the message-level `label_why`, then, per framing proposition (at most two shown), the `false_element` — the judge's quote from the message — its proposition-level `label_why`, and the CoT lines q3 cited for the reason not being the operative one. Run directories are in the appendix.
